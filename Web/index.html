            Credits

            AERODRIVE is an original, open-world driving simulator built with Three.js (rendering) and Rapier (physics).

            All 3D geometry, textures, and sounds in this build are generated procedurally by the game itself. No third-party models, maps, or audio are bundled.

            Inspired by the freedom and simulation spirit of modern driving sandboxes. Contains no assets, code, or branding from any commercial title.
